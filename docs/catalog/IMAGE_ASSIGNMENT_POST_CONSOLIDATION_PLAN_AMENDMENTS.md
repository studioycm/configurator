## 2. Remaining UX and recovery decision — now confirmed

Definitions:
- Next/Previous move the image inspector to a Product/Group in Results or Checked; they are separate from list pagination.
- If an image draft exists, Save & continue saves that record's image and opens the pending record. Discard & continue abandons only that image draft; Stay keeps it.
- These navigation actions belong to Images, not normal Product/Group editor pages.
- A lost Save response means the browser did not receive confirmation; it does not mean an image file was lost.

The human selected: after an uncertain image-only Save, check the saved image and continue if it matches.

Required outcome contract:
- Known successful write: “Image saved” and normal postcommit completion.
- Fresh authorized inspection finds the intended image state: “Image matches your choice”; the already-requested continuation may finish after owner/target/current draft/destination checks.
- Different image, changed/unavailable context or insufficient evidence: retain draft and stay, explain the conflict.
- Inspection does not save/replay, identify who wrote the image or prove that a particular request committed. Replace misleading committed/not_committed attribution in both service results and consumers with truthful goal/baseline/unknown outcomes.
- A baseline observation may permit an explicit fresh attempt under current validation; it is not rollback evidence or automatic retry permission.
- Native full-record Save/Create stays review_required when uncertain: image state cannot confirm metadata/settings. Unknown Group creation is never replayed automatically.
- Check the observed owner state again at completion or consume a matching fresh fingerprint so another writer between inspection and continuation cannot be silently ignored.
- Keep the existing authenticated preparation receipt; do not introduce a new generic durable Save-operation platform.

Focused later cases: keep with no Save invoked; remove on an already-empty owner with no Save; own successful assignment with lost response; another writer satisfying the same image goal; another writer changing it between inspection/completion; deleted/reassigned owner/destination; newer local draft. Assert notifications/events/reset/navigation and zero writes during inspection, not just returned labels.
